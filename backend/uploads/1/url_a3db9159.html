About HighWire

Over 75 years of combined experience in shaping industry

Born out of Stanford University, HighWire is a trusted global provider of innovative scholarly publishing services. In 1995, we spearheaded the digital publishing revolution, working with Google to digitize and index academic journals. Today, we are powered by MPS, providing the scholarly publishing community with leading edge solutions and capabilities.

Our end-to-end suite of platform tools span the entire publishing lifecycle: from manuscript submission and peer review, content hosting and publication, to analytics and reporting. Whether it’s a journal, preprint repository, or books and multimedia, we help you connect the brightest minds with the most people.
